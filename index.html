WHAT IS CYBERCRIME?

Cybercrime refers to any criminal activity that involves a computer or a network. This includes traditional crimes committed via the internet, as well as novel crimes unique to cyberspace.

Did You Know?

Between September 2024 and September 2025, Eswatini lost over E8.5 million to cybercrime, a 2.6% increase from the prior year, with mobile money scams accounting for E7.3 million of those losses[reference:0].

HOW, WHERE & PUNISHMENT

Aspect Details
How it Happens Phishing emails, malicious links, unpatched software, weak passwords, social engineering, and using public Wi-Fi without protection[reference:1].
Where it Occurs Social media, mobile money platforms, online banking, email inboxes, e-commerce sites, and even government portals[reference:2].
Punishment (Eswatini) Minor offences: fines up to E100,000 or 1–2 years imprisonment. Serious offences (e.g., cyberterrorism): fines up to E5 million and up to 20 years imprisonment[reference:3]. Unauthorized access can lead to 10 years imprisonment or a E10 million fine[reference:4].
