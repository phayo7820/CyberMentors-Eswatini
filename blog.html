Real Cases & Punishments

Case Study: Eswatini

Incident: A 25-year-old woman was fined E9,000 for posting a man's image on social media without his consent, breaching the Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act No.6 of 2022[reference:16].

Lesson: Even seemingly minor actions like sharing images without consent can have legal consequences. Digital responsibility is crucial.

Case Study: Global

Incident: A Malaysian national was sentenced to 5.5 years in prison in Singapore for teaching a criminal syndicate how to hack a bank, even though he didn't hack it himself[reference:17].

Lesson: Facilitating cybercrime is a serious offense. Ignorance of the law is not a defence, and international cooperation is increasing.
