Cybercrime: How It Happens & Punishments in Eswatini

How Cybercrime Happens

  • Phishing: Deceptive emails or messages that trick you into revealing sensitive information[reference:11].
  • Malware: Malicious software like viruses or ransomware that damages or locks your system[reference:12].
  • Social Engineering: Manipulating people into breaking security procedures.
  • Unsecured Networks: Using public Wi-Fi without protection can expose your data.

Punishments Under Eswatini Law

Minor Offences (e.g., harassment, cyberbullying): Fines up to E100,000 or 1–2 years imprisonment[reference:13].

Serious Offences (e.g., cyberterrorism, child pornography): Fines up to E5 million and imprisonment up to 20 years[reference:14].

Illegal Access/Data Espionage: Up to 10 years imprisonment or a E10 million fine[reference:15].
